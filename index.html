01 / Vision

Software can become meaningful.

We believe the future of digital companions is not only about producing better responses. It is about creating systems capable of developing meaningful, lasting relationships with the people who care for them.

02 / Flagship

Meet Nyxthera.

Nyxthera is an original digital companion species designed around consistent personality, meaningful memory, evolving behaviour, and long-term relationship.
