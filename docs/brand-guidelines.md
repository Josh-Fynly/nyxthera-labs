Nyxthera Labs Brand Guidelines

Brand

Name: Nyxthera Labs

Tagline: We build digital companions worth caring for.

Core philosophy: The relationship is the product.

Supporting principle: Build with logic. Design with empathy. Ship with purpose.

Positioning

Nyxthera Labs is a technology company focused on building digital companions and the systems that make long-term human–digital relationships possible.

The company should be presented as an engineering-led product laboratory, not as a generic AI company.

Product

Flagship product: Nyxthera

Nyxthera is a digital companion designed around persistent identity, state, memory, behaviour, growth, and relationship.

The Nyxthera product is developed separately from the Nyxthera Labs company website.

Visual Direction

The visual language combines:

- Deep space
- Engineered systems
- Living light
- Editorial restraint
- Technical precision
- Human-oriented product design

The interface should feel deliberate, quiet, precise, and technologically mature.

Avoid:

- Generic AI aesthetics
- Excessive gradients
- Heavy glassmorphism
- Excessive neon
- Decorative particle fields
- Unnecessary animations
- Crowded layouts
- Stock-photo-driven visual identity
- Template-like SaaS layouts

Colour Usage

Cyan is the primary signal colour.

Use it for:

- Interactive emphasis
- Active states
- Small visual signals
- Key interface accents
- Important calls to action

Violet represents depth and secondary visual emphasis.

Do not allow either accent to dominate the interface.

Large surfaces should remain dark and relatively quiet.

Typography

Primary font

Inter

Used for:

- Body text
- Navigation
- Interface text
- Supporting content

Heading font

Space Grotesk

Used for:

- Headings
- Major statements
- Brand-facing typography

Monospace font

JetBrains Mono

Used selectively for:

- Technical labels
- System terminology
- Small metadata
- Engineering-oriented details

Typography should provide hierarchy through size, weight, spacing, and contrast rather than excessive decoration.

Layout

The website should prioritize:

- Strong whitespace
- Clear hierarchy
- Wide content areas
- Consistent alignment
- Controlled density
- Responsive layouts

The primary content width is approximately 1200px.

Layouts should remain usable from small mobile screens through large desktop displays.

Motion

Motion should communicate system behaviour rather than exist purely for decoration.

Preferred motion characteristics:

- Subtle
- Slow where atmospheric
- Fast where interactive
- Predictable
- Purposeful

Respect "prefers-reduced-motion".

Avoid continuous high-frequency animation that distracts from content or unnecessarily consumes resources.

Interface Principles

Relationship First

The product experience should treat the relationship between person and companion as a core system rather than a cosmetic layer.

State Over Simulation

Persistent state, memory, behaviour, and continuity should matter more than superficial simulation.

Systems With Purpose

Engineering complexity should exist to support a meaningful product capability.

Do not add infrastructure, dependencies, or abstractions without a concrete reason.

Imagery

Imagery should support the product's conceptual world.

Preferred characteristics:

- Dark environments
- Controlled light
- Deep blue and violet depth
- Cyan points of illumination
- Abstract systems
- Organic-meets-engineered forms

Avoid generic AI imagery and unrelated stock photography.

Voice

Nyxthera Labs should communicate with:

- Precision
- Confidence
- Restraint
- Technical clarity
- Human awareness

Avoid:

- Empty hype
- Excessive startup jargon
- Artificial urgency
- Unsupported claims
- Overpromising
- Generic AI marketing language

Website Principles

The public website should be:

- Fast
- Accessible
- Indexable
- Responsive
- Content-oriented
- SEO-friendly
- AdSense-friendly
- Lightweight
- Easy to maintain

The website should not introduce authentication, dashboards, pricing systems, backend infrastructure, CMS dependencies, or fabricated product interactions unless there is a concrete product requirement for them.

Source of Truth

The CSS design tokens in "css/variables.css" are the implementation-level source of truth for colours, typography, spacing, radii, shadows, transitions, and layout dimensions.

Changes to the visual system should be reflected consistently across the documentation and implementation.
