Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Overview

Nyxthera Labs respects your privacy. This policy explains the information practices of the current Nyxthera Labs website and describes what happens when you visit the site.

The website is currently an informational, static website. It does not provide user accounts, payment processing, contact forms, newsletter subscriptions, or other features designed to collect information directly from visitors.

Information We Collect

Nyxthera Labs does not currently operate a user account system or intentionally collect personal information through forms, registrations, purchases, or subscriptions on this website.

Like most websites, the infrastructure used to deliver the site may process technical information associated with web requests, such as IP addresses, browser information, device information, timestamps, and requested resources. Such processing may be performed by hosting or infrastructure providers as part of operating and securing the website.

Third-Party Services

The website currently loads fonts from Google Fonts. When these resources are requested, the visitor's browser connects to Google's infrastructure to retrieve the requested font resources. Google may process technical information associated with those requests according to its own policies.

Nyxthera Labs does not currently integrate analytics platforms, advertising networks, payment processors, social-login providers, or third-party form services into this website.

How Information Is Used

Information processed through the website's hosting and infrastructure is used as necessary to deliver, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the website.

Nyxthera Labs does not currently use this website to build advertising profiles, sell visitor information, or operate targeted advertising.

Cookies and Tracking

The current Nyxthera Labs website does not intentionally set cookies for analytics, advertising, or user profiling.

Third-party infrastructure or browser-level technologies may operate according to their own policies and technical requirements.

External Links

The website may eventually link to external websites, applications, repositories, or services. Nyxthera Labs is not responsible for the privacy practices of external services. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those services before providing personal information.

Future Changes

Nyxthera Labs may introduce additional website features, products, services, analytics, communications, or integrations in the future. If those changes materially affect how information is collected or used, this policy will be updated to reflect the relevant practices.

Contact

If you need to contact Nyxthera Labs about this privacy policy or the website's information practices, an appropriate contact channel will be published here when one is established.
